Organization

The PharmaDS Collective (PDC) is a community of data science professionals working in the pharmaceutical industry, focused on building a strong and inclusive network addressing real-world data challenges in pharma.

PDC will be a leading platform in Indian pharma for developing talent and sharing practical approaches to data science. The group will help raise standards and encourage continuous learning, with a strong focus on data science, including AI and machine learning.
